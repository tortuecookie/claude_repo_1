Module 5

MVM in Practice

Worked numerical examples for a life annuity portfolio and a non-life reserve portfolio, with sensitivity analysis and commentary on typical MVM magnitudes.

Example A: Life Annuity Portfolio

Portfolio Characteristics
  • Number of annuitants: 10,000 lives (average age 72)
  • Annual annuity payment: CHF 20,000 per life
  • Expected run-off: 25 years
  • BEL at valuation date: CHF 2,000m
  • Risk-free yield curve: approximately flat at 2% p.a.
  • SCRMVM(0) for longevity risk: CHF 120m (6% of BEL)

Result: Life Annuity MVM

Sum of all discounted SCRMVM(t) values ≈ CHF 920m

MVM = 6% × 920m = CHF 55.2m

As a percentage of BEL: 55.2 / 2,000 = 2.76%

MCVL = BEL + MVM = 2,000 + 55.2 = CHF 2,055.2m

Duration Cross-Check

Modified duration of annuity cash flows ≈ 8.5 years at 2% yield.

Duration approximation: MVM ≈ 6% × 120m × 8.5 = CHF 61.2m (within ~11% of full calc).

Key Takeaways — Module 5
  • Life annuity example (BEL = CHF 2bn, 25-year horizon): MVM ≈ CHF 55m (2.76% of BEL).
  • Non-life reserve example (BEL = CHF 500m, 12-year horizon): MVM ≈ CHF 18m (3.52% of BEL).
  • MVM / BEL is driven primarily by the SCRMVM / BEL ratio and the run-off duration.
  • MVM is linearly proportional to the CoC rate.
  • In a low interest-rate environment, MVM tends to be higher.
  • Typical MVM / BEL: under 1% (short-tail savings) to over 7% (long-tail liability reinsurance).
