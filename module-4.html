Module 4

MVM Calculation Methodology

A step-by-step walkthrough of the full MVM calculation, from identifying the run-off profile to applying the cost-of-capital formula and approved simplifications.

The Full Formula

MVM — Definitive Formula (SST)
MVM = CoC × Σt=0T−1 SCRMVM(t) / (1 + r0,t+1)t+1
CoC                 = 0.06 (6% per annum)
SCRMVM(t) = non-hedgeable risk capital at beginning of year t+1
r0,t+1           = risk-free spot rate from today to time t+1
T                     = run-off horizon

Step-by-Step Calculation Guide

Step 1: Define the Liability Portfolio

Identify all insurance liabilities and determine the run-off horizon T (the year SCRMVM reaches zero).

Step 2: Compute BEL and SCRMVM(0)

Calculate the Best Estimate and the current-year non-hedgeable capital requirement. This is the standard SST calculation restricted to underwriting and reinsurance credit risk (no market risk).

Step 3: Project SCRMVM(t) for t = 1 to T−1

ApproachMethodAccuracyEffort
Full projectionRe-run SST underwriting risk model year by yearHighestVery high
Proportional methodSCRMVM(t) = SCRMVM(0) × BEL(t)/BEL(0)ModerateLow
Duration approximationMVM ≈ CoC × SCRMVM(0) × DLRoughVery low
FINMA guidance: Larger and more complex insurers are expected to use a projection-based approach. Smaller companies may apply proportional or duration-based simplifications provided they can demonstrate it does not materially understate the MVM.

Step 4: Apply Discount Factors

Obtain the FINMA-prescribed risk-free spot rates and compute:

Discount Factor
vt+1 = 1 / (1 + r0,t+1)t+1

Step 5: Sum the Discounted Capital Charges

Final Assembly
MVM = 0.06 × [ SCRMVM(0)×v1 + SCRMVM(1)×v2 + ... + SCRMVM(T−1)×vT ]

The Proportional Simplification in Detail

Proportional Simplification
SCRMVM(t) = SCRMVM(0) × BEL(t) / BEL(0)
Valid when the risk profile does not change materially as the portfolio shrinks.
MVM with Proportional Simplification
MVM = 0.06 × [ SCRMVM(0) / BEL(0) ] × Σt=0T−1 BEL(t) × vt+1

The Duration Approximation

Duration Approximation (rough cross-check only)
MVM ≈ CoC × SCRMVM(0) × DL
DL = modified (Macaulay) duration of the BEL cash flows

Common Pitfalls

  • Including market risk in SCRMVM — overstates MVM.
  • Using the wrong yield curve — must use FINMA risk-free rates.
  • Ignoring long-tail lines — run-off can extend 20–40 years; cutting it short materially understates MVM.
  • Proportional simplification on volatile lines — if SCR/BEL is not stable, simplification introduces bias.
Key Takeaways — Module 4
  • MVM = 6% × Σ SCRMVM(t) / (1 + rt+1)t+1 summed from t=0 to T−1.
  • The most demanding step is projecting SCRMVM(t) for all future years.
  • The proportional simplification assumes SCRMVM declines in line with BEL.
  • The duration approximation (MVM ≈ 6% × SCRMVM(0) × DL) is a rough cross-check only.
  • Market risk and operational risk must be excluded from SCRMVM.
  • Use FINMA-prescribed risk-free discount rates consistent with BEL valuation.
